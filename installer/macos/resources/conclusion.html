LPS is installed

LPS is starting and the setup wizard opens in your browser at http://127.0.0.1:7070. It checks Docker and creates your admin account.

LPS needs Docker: Docker Desktop, OrbStack or Colima.

LPS starts whenever you sign in; turn that off on the dashboard, or with lpsd autostart off in Terminal. To remove LPS, run sudo lps-uninstall in Terminal.
